FAQ

Know what this checker does — and doesn’t do.

Does this tell me Cintas overcharged me?

No. It identifies objective changes between invoices. Whether a charge is permitted may depend on your service agreement, amendments or service changes.

What files work best?

Original digital invoice PDFs with selectable text. Scans and phone photos are not supported in this validation version.

Do you store my invoices?

Not in this V0. The source PDFs are parsed locally in your browser and are not intentionally transmitted to our server.

Is this affiliated with Cintas?

No. This is an independent comparison tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Cintas Corporation.
